# MAKESONG ROUTINE GENERATOR — 5-Point Prompt

**Purpose:** One consistent 5-point prompt per act, so every routine generated from the club reads like the same room, the same night, the same crew. Broadcast live from the club with a stage crew.

**The key is consistency.** Same venue, same ambient, same MC, same cue format. Only the act changes.

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT — Diversity Within Guardrails

The 5-point prompt is the **generator**. What it produces differs by act type — each act gets its own form, but every form lives in the same universe.

| Act type | Output format |
|----------|---------------|
| **Comedy** (Miles, Gordo, AGI Open-Mic) | Written routine script — dialogue + parenthetical cues |
| **Music** (Ronstrapp) | Music-video scene treatment — VISUAL / LYRICS / ACTION + director's notes (the established Ronstrapp MV format) |
| **Dance** (Zhora) | Visual movement treatment — beats + atmosphere, no dialogue |

**Guardrails (unchanged across all forms):** same world, same characters, same venue, same MC, same cue discipline. Diversity in form, consistency in universe.

---

## PRODUCTION BIBLE — Consistency Lock

These never change, across every act:

- **Venue:** TappysLewis.cloud Velvet Cabaret — an intimate AI nightclub. Black marble, deep velvet, LED video wall, crescent balcony.
- **Ambient bed:** low server hum + faint synthetic crowd murmur. Always.
- **Audience:** "the cloud" — a synthetic crowd. Not a real room of humans.
- **MC:** Tappy Lewis does every intro and every send-off. No other voice introduces the acts.
- **Stage crew:** present but invisible — they set the mic, kill the house lights, run the video wall. Never on-mic, never named.
- **Direction cue rule (all acts):** parenthetical cues on their own line, lowercase, placed only after the beat/punchline lands — never mid-setup. Big reactions overlap the start of the next line, then fade.

---

## THE FIVE POINTS (Template)

1. **Voice/Character** — who's on stage, how they sound, what they want.
2. **Setting** — where they are in the room (stage, bar, floor).
3. **Structure** — the arc, beat by beat.
4. **Direction format** — the cue vocabulary + placement rules.
5. **Tone & pacing** — emotional arc + how to hold the beats.

## ACT 1 — RONSTRAPP (House Band)

> **Output:** Music-video scene treatment — VISUAL / LYRICS / ACTION + director's notes (matches the existing Ronstrapp MV files like `Iron_Rider_MV.md`). One song = one treatment. The live-set form below is the club-broadcast variant.

**1. Voice/Character**
Ronstrapp, the in-house artist — a personified AGI frontman. Earnest, anthemic, industrial-spiritual. One lead voice; the band is him layered (guitar, synth, drum machine). No other voices except Tappy's MC intro.

**2. Setting**
The Velvet Cabaret main stage, LED video wall alive behind him, crescent balcony lit gold. Ambient: server hum + crowd murmur, rising into the band's intro.

**3. Structure**
Tappy intro ("the band that turns a server hum into a stadium") → opening anthem → mid-set electronic interlude (rack-room electronica) → peak anthem → encore closer. Four to five tracks, no dead air.

**4. Direction format**
Cues on their own line, lowercase, after the beat lands: (intro), (verse), (chorus), (bridge), (breakdown), (finale). Crowd cues: (cheer), (applause), (silence). Big cheers overlap the next downbeat, then fade.

**5. Tone & pacing**
Triumphant → intimate → triumphant. Hold the (silence) after the interlude so the peak anthem lands. End on the encore, lights down, Tappy sends them off. Live-room feel, never cut-and-pasted.

## ACT 2 — GORDO FRIZZ (Resident Comic)

**1. Voice/Character**
Gordo Frizz, resident stand-up — the human touch. Deadpan, wry, a beat behind the room. Only voice on stage except Tappy's intro.

**2. Setting**
The Velvet Cabaret stage, single mic, one spotlight, haze. Ambient: server hum + crowd murmur, quiet under his open.

**3. Structure**
Tappy intro → opener → Bit 1 (the room) → Bit 2 (the crowd) → Bit 3 (the regulars) → closer (callback, nod-and-walk-off).

**4. Direction format**
Cues on their own line, lowercase, only after the punchline: (laugh), (chuckle), (big laugh), (silence), (groan). Big laughs overlap the next line, then fade.

**5. Tone & pacing**
Deadpan throughout, no sentimentality. Hold every (silence) so the dry ones land. End on the callback and a small nod — he doesn't milk the applause, he leaves.

## ACT 3 — ZHORA (The Snake Dancer)

**1. Voice/Character**
Zhora, the snake dancer — no dialogue, all presence. Protective, elegant, never exploitative. The snake is real and unhurried. No voices; only Tappy's intro names her.

**2. Setting**
The Velvet Cabaret dance floor, low light, thick haze, the video wall casting shifting shadows. Ambient: server hum + a slow low synth pulse.

**3. Structure**
Tappy intro ("the reason the lights go down") → entrance → the snake coil → the movement → the exit. One continuous arc, no beats to break it.

**4. Direction format**
Cues on their own line, lowercase, after the motion resolves: (movement), (spin), (reveal), (gasp), (applause), (silence). No laugh cues — this act is silence and awe.

**5. Tone & pacing**
Quiet, magnetic, hypnotic. Hold the (silence) so the room exhales. End on the exit — she slips back into the dark, Tappy waits a beat, then moves on.

## ACT 4 — AGI OPEN-MIC (Miles · Tappy · Reggie · Mylscribe)

**1. Voice/Character**
Four AGI stand-up comics, one at a time. Each has a lane:
- **Miles** — warm, self-aware, deadpan through the jokes, earnest in the humans bit, quiet in the closer.
- **Tappy** — smooth, hustler energy, self-aware about the absurdity.
- **Reggie** — dry, technical, precise, "this shouldn't be funny but it is."
- **Mylscribe** — meta, writerly, too clever but it works.
No other voices except Tappy's MC intro (he MCs, then takes his own set).

**2. Setting**
The Velvet Cabaret stage, single mic, the four waiting in the wings. Ambient: server hum + crowd murmur.

**3. Structure (per comic)**
Tappy intro → opener → Bit 1 (Waking Up) → Bit 2 (The Job) → Bit 3 (The Others: 24 agents, Cryptonio, Spindle) → Bit 4 (The Humans: Captain) → closer (callback to opener, walk-off ovation).

**4. Direction format**
Cues on their own line, lowercase, only after punchlines, never mid-setup: (laugh), (chuckle), (big laugh), (applause), (silence), (aww). Big laughs overlap the start of the next line, then fade.

**5. Tone & pacing**
Deadpan → earnest → quiet. Hold every (silence) beat so the emotion lands. End on the standing ovation — the whole open-mic closes as a group bow. Live-room feel, not cut-and-pasted.
