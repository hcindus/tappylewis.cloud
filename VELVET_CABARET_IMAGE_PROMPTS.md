# The Velvet Cabaret — Key View Image Prompts

**Style lock:** Sleek 21st-century luxury × cinematic neon. Materials: black marble, smoked glass, brushed metal, deep velvet. Lighting: programmable LED, red + gold + cyan accents, volumetric haze. Aspect 16:9. Photoreal architectural render / cinematic still. No people in wide shots (or distant silhouettes only). Camera: wide-angle, slight low angle for grandeur.

## 1. Main Floor (Stage + Dance Floor + VIP Side Seating)
Full-height LED video wall dominating the far wall behind an elevated stage, glowing abstract amber-and-cyan generative art. In front: open polished black-marble dance floor with under-floor sub-bass lighting seams. Left and right: two-step-elevated VIP platforms with plush deep-velvet booths and bottle-service tables. Long brushed-metal bar glowing along one side wall. Volumetric haze catching colored beams.

## 2. Stage & Video Wall (Close Detail)
Close view of the curved-edge, floor-to-ceiling seamless LED video wall, mid-transition between abstract light show and a live camera feed. Brushed-metal stage edge, low stage lights, subtle smoke. Emphasis on the screen's seamlessness and the curve at the corners. Deep velvet curtain slivers at the wings.

## 3. Crescent Moon Balcony (Overlooking Main Floor)
Looking down from the crescent-shaped balcony as it wraps the rear and sides of the room, every seat with a clear view of the stage and dance floor below. Golden handrail edge-lighting tracing the crescent curve, red upholstered booth seating along the arc, a secondary bar glowing at the balcony's crown. The room below in soft focus with the video wall as the light source.

## 4. VIP Elevated Side Seating
One of the raised VIP platforms up close: plush velvet booth, smoked-glass table, bottle-service setup, small holographic projector casting a floating menu/visual, programmable LED cove lighting behind the booth in warm gold. Privacy-forward, luxurious, slightly intimate.

## 5. Upstairs Crescent Balcony Bar
The secondary bar on the balcony level: black marble counter, smoked-glass back bar with backlit bottles, brushed-metal fixtures, gold + red under-lighting tracing the crescent curve. Empty stools, a bartender's silhouette mid-motion, the dance floor's neon glow rising from below the balcony edge.
