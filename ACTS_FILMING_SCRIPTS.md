# The Velvet Cabaret — 5-Point Filming Scripts

**Purpose:** Cinematic, reusable footage for each act. 5 shots per act = a tight loop that can stand alone as a promo or cut into a longer reel. Shoot 16:9 for the main film, 9:16 reframe for social.

**Global style:** Black marble + deep velvet, red/gold/cyan neon, volumetric haze, LED video wall as a living backdrop. Slow push-ins, shallow depth of field, never overcut — let the room breathe.

---

## ACT 1 — RONSTRAPP (House Band)

| # | Shot | Action | Audio |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Wide, low angle** | Establish the stage: band in silhouette, LED wall blazing behind them, haze catching the beams | Opening chord + sub-bass thump |
| 2 | **Tight insert** | Fingers on guitar neck / drumstick strike / vocalist leaning into the mic — one hero detail | Instrument isolate |
| 3 | **Dance floor wide** | Crowd moving, LED floor seams pulsing in sync with the beat | Full band mix |
| 4 | **Mid, stage right** | The video wall mid-transition — abstract art morphing to a live feed as the song builds | Build into chorus |
| 5 | **Wide, lights-out** | Final chord, blackout, then the "RONSTRAPP" neon tag flashes on the wall | Cut to silence + reverb tail |

*Cue tracks: "Ashes of Bayou" (metal opener), "Rack Room Reverie" (electronic interlude), "Uptime Gospel" (finale).*

## ACT 2 — GORDO FRIZZ (Resident Comic)

| # | Shot | Action | Audio |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Wide, empty stage** | Single mic stand, one spotlight, haze — the club before the joke | Room ambience, low murmur |
| 2 | **Medium** | Gordo steps to the mic, adjusts it, deadpan opener | "Good crowd tonight. For a room full of replicants." |
| 3 | **Tight, reaction** | Gordo's face mid-pause — the beat before the punchline | Silence / held breath |
| 4 | **Crowd cutaway** | Patrons laughing, glasses raising, one guy wiping a tear | Laughter swell |
| 5 | **Medium, walk-off** | Punchline lands, Gordo gives a small nod, walks off into the dark | Laughter fading + a beat |

*Gordo is the human touch — shoot him warm, not clinical. One take energy (Hy Pyke nod).*

## ACT 3 — ZHORA (The Snake Dancer)

| # | Shot | Action | Audio |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Wide, floor** | The dance floor in low light, a single figure and the snake, haze thick | Slow, low synth pulse |
| 2 | **Tight, the snake** | The snake coiling around her arm — real, unhurried | Minimal percussion |
| 3 | **Medium, movement** | Zhora mid-motion, fabric + light, the video wall casting shifting shadows | Rising, breathy |
| 4 | **Cutaway, Taffey** | Taffey at the bar, power position, watching — approving, unreadable | Bar clink, distant |
| 5 | **Wide, exit** | She finishes and slips back into the dark; the room exhales | Music drops to silence |

*Protective, elegant, never exploitative — she's the reason the lights go down. No dialogue, all atmosphere.*

## ACT 4 — AGI OPEN-MIC (Tappy · Miles · Reggie · Mylscribe)

| # | Shot | Action | Audio |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | **Wide, stage** | Four mics (or one rotating), the comics waiting in the wings, room full | Low crowd buzz |
| 2 | **Tight, each comic** | Quick-cut: each comic delivers their signature line, punchline-first | Laughter per beat |
| 3 | **Crowd cutaway** | Genuine reaction — the "gotcha" laugh, agents + humans both | Laughter swell |
| 4 | **Two-shot, callback** | The callback moment — a setup paid off from earlier, comics glancing at each other | The groan + the laugh |
| 5 | **Wide, group bow** | All four step forward, bow, "Goodnight, I have 12 hours of memory left." | Applause + fade |

*Shoot each comic's lead bit separately, then intercut. Keep it KISS — punchlines land, no explaining the joke.*

## Usage Notes
- Each act's 5 shots = one self-contained ~15–30s vertical cut for TikTok/Reels.
- Full 16:9 versions chain together for a "One Night at the Velvet Cabaret" reel.
- Ronstrapp + AGI Open-Mic are the content engines (music + comedy). Gordo + Zhora are the atmosphere and canon depth.
- Cover frames: the neon sign (TAPPY LEWIS / VELVET CABARET) or the video-wall wide shot.
