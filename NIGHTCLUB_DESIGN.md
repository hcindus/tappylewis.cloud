# Tappy Lewis — Nightclub Venue Design (21st Century Concept)

**Status:** Design brief — reference for the club's physical/architectural vision
**Style:** Sleek 21st-century luxury × cinematic neon × immersive tech

## Concept Names
- **The Velvet Cabaret** (primary name — Tappy approved)
- Alt: Luna or Eclipse (playing on the crescent-moon balcony)

## Branding / Neon Sign
- **Name:** "TAPPY LEWIS" big on top, "THE VELVET CABARET" in red neon below.
- **Online / Offline:** "Tappy Lewis Online" during business hours, "Tappy Lewis Offline" when closed. One sign, two moods.
- **Velvet** = Tappy's signature "Velvet Touch" (from SOUL.md). **Cabaret** = show, class, entertainment.
- **Lore:** The Snake Pit = where Tappy cut his teeth (door/bar). The Velvet Cabaret = where he runs the room.

## Material Palette
Dark, sophisticated: black marble, smoked glass, brushed metal, deep velvet — contrasted with dynamic lighting and massive digital displays.

## Layout

### Main Floor
- **Stage** — large rectangular, far end, elevated ~1–1.2 m for sightlines.
- **Video wall** — seamless floor-to-ceiling high-res LED panel (slightly curved edges) covering the entire stage back wall. Displays live camera feeds, generative art, music videos, abstract light shows, branded content.
- **Flex space** — directly in front of stage, converts between full dance floor ↔ seated show (tables/chairs added or removed quickly).
- **VIP floor seating** — elevated platforms (2–3 steps) left + right of main floor; plush booths, lounge seating, private bottle-service tables.
- **Bar** — full-service, running one long side wall (or central island depending on footprint).

### Upper Level
- **Crescent balcony** — wraps rear + sides, every seat views stage + dance floor. Dramatic architectural statement, improves acoustics/sightlines.
- **Secondary bar** — full-service, on the balcony level.
- **VIP booths** — private booths + lounge seating along the crescent curve.

## Amenities
- State-of-the-art sound: distributed speakers + sub-bass arrays under the dance floor.
- Programmable LED architectural lighting (walls, ceiling coves, handrails, under-balcony edges).
- Multiple small LED screens / holographic projectors in VIP areas.
- High-end restrooms, green room/backstage, coat check, discreet VIP entrance.
- Climate control with subtle scent diffusion + excellent air circulation.

- Flexible main floor (dance nights ↔ seated shows)
- Strong VIP hierarchy
- Crescent balcony + immersive video wall as architectural centerpieces
