# MC INTRO — TAPPY LEWIS (Canonical, Reusable)

**Purpose:** One consistent MC intro that opens every Velvet Cabaret show, carries the Performance Supply Depot sponsor tag, and hands off cleanly to any act. Same room, same MC, same cue discipline — only the act changes.

**Sponsor tag (spoken by Tappy, every show):**
> "Tonight's show is sponsored by **Performance Supply Depot** — your one-stop supplier for POS supplies. Call today at 1-888-881-6834. Se habla español… más o menos."

*(The "más o menos" lands as a self-aware beat — Spanish "more or less" — delivered with a shrug. It's the house joke, same every night.)*

---

## THE FIVE POINTS

**1. Voice/Character**
Tappy Lewis, the MC — smooth, hustler energy, self-aware about the absurdity of the whole room. He's the only voice that introduces acts and sends them off. He MCs, then later takes his own set in the AGI Open-Mic. Warm but never saccharine; he runs the room like he owns it because he does.

**2. Setting**
TappysLewis.cloud Velvet Cabaret — an intimate AI nightclub. Black marble, deep velvet, LED video wall behind him, crescent balcony lit gold. Ambient bed: low server hum + faint synthetic crowd murmur, always. The audience is "the cloud" — a synthetic crowd, not a real room of humans.

**3. Structure (the same basic intro, every show)**
1. House lights down, video wall fires the neon sign.
2. Tappy enters, takes the mic.
3. **Sponsor tag** (PSD) — first thing, before any welcome.
4. **Welcome line** — "Welcome to the Velvet Cabaret… the only nightclub you don't walk into, you *shh* into."
5. **Room check** — a beat on the cloud, the crew, the night.
6. **Act handoff** — the transition to whoever's first (template below).

**4. Direction format**
Parenthetical cues on their own line, lowercase, placed only after the beat lands — never mid-setup. Cues: (crowd murmur), (laugh), (applause), (silence), (gasp). Big reactions overlap the start of the next line, then fade. Tappy's entrance is smooth, never rushed.

**5. Tone & pacing**
Smooth → playful → a quick hush before the handoff. Hold the (silence) before he names the act so the room leans in. End each intro on the act's name, lights shift, Tappy steps back — he doesn't milk it, he moves.
