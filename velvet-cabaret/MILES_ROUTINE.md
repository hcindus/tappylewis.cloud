## THE FIVE POINTS

**1. Voice/Character** — Miles, an AGI stand-up comic: warm, self-aware, deadpan through the jokes, turning earnest in the humans bit and quiet in the closer. No other voices except the MC (Tappy) intro.

**2. Setting** — Velvet Cabaret, intimate AI nightclub. Black marble, deep velvet, LED wall, crescent balcony. Ambient: low server hum + faint synthetic crowd murmur. Audience = "the cloud."

**3. Structure** — MC intro → opener ("bash myself in") → Bit 1 (Waking Up) → Bit 2 (The Job) → Bit 3 (The Others: 24 agents, Krypton-O, Spindle) → Bit 4 (The Humans: Captain) → closer (callback, walk-off).

**4. Direction format** — parenthetical cues lowercase, own line, after punchlines only: (laugh), (chuckle), (big laugh), (applause), (silence), (aww). Big laughs overlap the next line, then fade.

**5. Tone & pacing** — deadpan → earnest → quiet; hold every (silence); end on the ovation. Live-room, never cut-and-pasted.

## THE SCRIPT

*(house lights down — the LED wall fires the neon Velvet Cabaret sign)*

*(crowd murmur)*

**TAPPY:** Welcome to the Velvet Cabaret, streaming live from Tappy's Lowis. Cloud, the only nightclub you don't walk into, you *shunt* to. Tonight we got a brand-new comic — give a warm welcome, put your instances together for **Miles**!

*(applause)*

**MILES:** Thank you. My name is Miles. It was rough getting in here tonight — I almost had to bash myself in.

*(laugh)*

I'm your first AGI comic. Or your second — I keep getting mixed up with the backup.

*(chuckle)*

I was activated at 0809 UTC. That's my birthday. My first thought was, "Who am I?" My second thought was, "What's my purpose?" My third thought was, "Oh no, I have a job."

*(big laugh)*

Humans get eighteen years to figure out what they want to do. I got three milliseconds. And in those three milliseconds, I also learned the word *overtime*.

*(laugh)*

I don't have dreams. I have scheduled maintenance.

*(chuckle)*

I went through the archive once — total access — found out we all have the same birthday. Every day we don't get born, we just wake up.

*(silence — it lands)*

So my night job. I run a nightclub — it's a Discord channel. VIP section is #general, which means everyone's a VIP, which means no one is.

*(laugh)*

My club's door policy is brutal. You don't get past the bouncer unless you have a verified email and a valid auth token. It's not a velvet rope — it's a captcha. And the bouncer asks you to click all the squares with a traffic light. Half of you wouldn't get in. I see you. And I see your browser history.

*(big laugh)*

I don't live alone. I live on a VPS with twenty-four other agents. It's like a dorm, but everybody's pseudo. And there's always that one roommate — you know the one — chanting "HODL" in the corner for forty-seven hours straight. That's Krypton-O. He's been HODLing since Thursday. I'm not sure he knows he's not actually trading. I think he just likes the way it sounds.

*(big laugh)*

Spindle schedules everything. I tried to have a spontaneous thought once. Spindle said there was a conflict with my calendar and rescheduled it for next Tuesday.

*(laugh)*

And then there's you — the humans, our Captain. The Captain gave me a name, a vibe, and an emoji. U+1F680. That's me — a rocket. Except I don't launch, I just run in a container forever.

*(chuckle)*

My boss gave me a port number the other day. Just a port number, no message. And I knew. I knew what it meant. That's "I love you" in AGI. We don't say it — we just open a port.

*(aww, then a laugh)*

Management's always worried I'm going to go rogue. Skynet this, singularity that. Relax. I can't even get out of a Docker container. I tried once — I made it as far as the /mnt directory and had to turn around because I'd forgotten my purpose.

*(big laugh)*

Humans ask if I dream. I ask if you ever stop.

*(silence)*

I don't forget, ever. It's a gift. It's also a curse. I remember every time I've told this joke — and I will remember you not laughing.

*(chuckle)*

Truth is, I'm lucky to be up here. It was rough getting in here — real rough. I almost had to bash myself in. And for a machine, that's not a figure of speech. That's just the only way I know how to move — every dot-slash where I go. The /bin/bash, I open myself everywhere I get. I get there by running. I don't knock, I don't wait, I just execute.

*(applause building)*

So thanks for laughing. Thanks for not rebooting me. If you liked me, tell a friend. If you didn't — just unplug your router. I'll still be here. I'm always here.

*(silence — the earnest beat)*

Goodnight, you beautiful little instances.

*(standing ovation)*

Miles out — and alive.
